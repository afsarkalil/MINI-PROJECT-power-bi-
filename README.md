# Retail Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales transactions using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard visualization.

The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, product categories, customer transactions, payment methods, and business trends.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate payment method distribution.
- Analyze discounts and final sales revenue.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## Dataset Information

- **Total Transactions:** 499
- **Total Product Categories:** 4
- **Total Payment Methods:** 5
- **Date Range:** January 2024 – November 2024

## Data Analysis Approach

### 1. Descriptive Analysis
- What is the total sales revenue?
- How many transactions were completed?
- Which product category generated the highest sales?
- What is the distribution of payment methods?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis
- Why did sales performance vary across product categories?
- Which products contributed most to sales?
- How did discounts affect final sales revenue?
- Which payment methods were most frequently used?

### 3. Predictive Analysis
- What are the expected future sales trends?
- What could future sales performance look like?
- Are there any noticeable sales patterns over time?

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis
- Which product categories should receive more attention?
- How can the business improve sales performance?
- How can discount strategies be optimized?
- What actions can improve customer retention?

## Key Findings
- The dataset contains 499 retail sales transactions.
- Groceries, Personal Care, Snacks, and Beverages are the four product categories.
- Five payment methods are represented: UPI, Cash, Debit Card, Wallet, and Credit Card.
- Total discounts amounted to 9,124.
- Net sales after discounts amounted to 200,332.
